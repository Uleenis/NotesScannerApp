# Architecture

The detailed contracts of the code: what each part does, its bounds, its answers and its refusals. `CLAUDE.md` keeps the map and the rules; read here the section of the part a branch touches, and update it in the same branch when a contract changes.

## Layout in detail

- `src/app/`: routes (Expo Router) and their `_layout.tsx`, nothing else.
- `src/components/`: shared components.
- `src/i18n/`: language files (`en.ts`, `fr.ts`) and the choice of language.
- `src/auth/`: the anonymous account, the provider that opens it when the app starts and follows every change of account (sign-up, sign-in, sign-out), and the actions of the account by e-mail code (`account.ts`, `account-errors.ts`, `account-service.ts`). A sign-in from an anonymous account that has pages checks its code on a second client that keeps nothing (`getStatelessSupabase`), calls `transfer-account` with both tokens, and only then gives the account found to the client of the phone.
- `src/lib/`: the Supabase client, its config, the secure session storage, the addresses of the terms and the privacy policy (`legal-links.ts`), the opening of a web address (`open-link.ts`) and the forgetting of the data of other accounts.
- `src/notebooks/`: reading, creating and caching notebooks.
- `src/capture/`: the limits shown before the camera and the hook that opens the camera or the limit sheet, sending a page (create, prepare, upload, mark it uploaded, start the reading), preparing the photo, the camera and network hooks. The photo is saved again in the sense of the viewfinder, never in the sense the phone guessed from gravity: the camera gives its orientation tag (`exif: true`), the app turns the image back to the viewfinder (plus the quarter turns of "Rotate"), shrinks it above 12 Mpx, and saves one JPEG with no metadata. The viewfinder and the check of the photo have the shape of the photo (3:4). A page only counts for the user (notebook counts, last notebook, page lists) once its `uploaded_at` is set, so a page created for a photo that never arrived stays invisible.
- `src/pages/`: the pages a user sees (list, one page with its number and text, signed address of its photo, starting or restarting a reading), what a page shows according to its status, the title of a page and the gluing of its cut lines at display time (the text itself is never changed), `clean.ts` (the one module that cleans a page of text for display: it removes `<u>` and `</u>` and no other tag, and a blank line between two non-empty lines of B1 unless B2, read by `second.ts` from `readings` with `label = 'B2'`, has a blank line between the lines that match them by content without case, accents, spaces or punctuation; a line without a match keeps its blank line, nothing is removed without B2, on a page that has gaps written by an edit, on a page of math, or above 1,000,000 pairs of lines; it only returns a text and the words moved to it, so no reading, place or position changes, and the Edit mode starts from that text and writes nothing when it is left unchanged), dates without `Intl`, and the polling delays. Every query here keeps to pages whose `uploaded_at` is set. `formulas.ts` cuts the body of a page of math into text and formulas (`$…$` on one line with no space inside its dollars, `$$…$$`, `\(…\)`, `\[…\]`, `\begin{…}…\end{…}`; anything unclosed stays text) and into rows; `tex.ts` draws one formula as an SVG string with MathJax 4 (`@mathjax/src` and its font, Apache-2.0) on the phone, with the TeX packages `base` and `ams` only (no `\href`, `\url`, `\class`, `\style`, `\require`), every font range it may need imported with the app so nothing is ever fetched, and an output with a link or an image refused; it refuses before drawing a formula over 2,000 characters, nested deeper than 20 (counted with what is open: a brace, a `\left` until its `\right`, an environment until its `\end`), using `\DeclareMathOperator` or `\pmb` (their nesting doubles the work), `\mmlToken` (MathML attributes), `\newcolumntype`, `\label`, `\tag`, `\ref`, `\eqref`, `\hsize`, `\vbox`, `\vtop`, `\vcenter` or `\parbox` (the last five start a slow line breaking), an environment outside the common ones (the matrices, `cases`, `aligned`, `align`, `gathered`, `gather`, `equation`, `split`, `multline`, `flalign`, `eqnarray`, `array`; so no `alignat` nor `subarray`), an `array` whose column template is not 1 to 20 columns `l`, `c` or `r` with at most one `|` around each, a `\begin` whose name cannot be read, or a `%` comment (it would hide what it closes from the count); after drawing it refuses an SVG over 512 KB, larger than 300 by 120 ex or 4,000 square ex, of more than 3,000 elements, or holding a link, an image or a `url(`. Each formula gets a fresh TeX input and a fresh SVG output, so nothing it defines or sets reaches the next; the drawing must be one root picture (inline line breaks are off). Formulas are drawn one at a time, each after a turn of the screen, a formula asked twice waits for the first drawing, a drawing still queued when its formula leaves the screen is dropped (`releaseTex`), a page draws at most 100 formulas and shows the rest as read, and the last 50 results are kept (the oldest goes first) and read at once. A formula it refuses is shown as it was read, never as an error. `words.ts` reads the positions (`page_tokens`) and corrections of a page by pages of 1,000 rows (20 reads at most, else the page is not editable) and places each word in the text the page shows: from the places in B1 (`b1_start`, `b1_end`, in characters) shifted by the length of every word a correction or a rule changed, checked against the text (a changed word that is not where it should be, a word without a place, overlapping places or a place past the end make the whole page not editable, never a guess); the suggestions of a word are its distinct readings of A, B1 and B2, in that order. `isWord` is the client's copy of the word rule of `corrections` (1 to 100 characters, none of the fixed list of spaces, controls and invisible characters), and `addCorrection` sends only `page_id`, `token_index`, `corrected_text`, `chosen_from` and `from_queue: false`, and nothing when the word fails `isWord`. `displayPieces` (`display.ts`) gives the same title and blocks as `displayDocument` as pieces that each know their place in the text, so a word can be found in the glued lines. The engine itself (`mathjax-engine.ts`) is loaded on the first formula, not at app start. On a page of math the first line is the title only when no formula starts on it; display formulas scroll sideways when wider than the screen, and an inline formula wider than 36 ex is shown as read. `mathjax-context.ts`, imported first, gives React Native's `navigator` the `appVersion` and `userAgent` MathJax reads at load. `metro.config.js` resolves the package subpath imports of MathJax (`#default-font/*` and kin), whose bare targets Metro 0.84 cannot follow. `edit.ts` is the "Modifier" mode: `alignEdit` takes the text shown, the placed words and the text the user wrote and answers `same`, `refused` (a text over 100,000 characters, more than 1,000 differences between the two lists of words, or more than 2,000 words changed) or the words and gaps to send; it matches the words of the two texts with the word rule of the service (the same regular expression, combining marks counted as letters) by a Myers difference, exact word for exact word (a position whose word holds a character outside that rule, kept by an earlier correction, is looked for whole in the new text first), pairs in order the words that changed inside a hole of the difference (a position left without a word is deleted, a word left without a position stays in the text between the words), keeps every other character (added words, line cuts, punctuation, spaces) as the text of the user in the gaps, and checks that the words and gaps give the text exactly back; `editPage` calls `edit-page`. `words.ts` reads `page_gaps` too (`gaps` in `PageWords`) and `placeWords` places each word after the gap that comes before it, a deleted word having no length (never tappable). `alerts.ts` reads the kinds of alert a page shows (`missing_line` on a page of text, `math_incomplete` on a page of math). `use-text-edit.ts` holds the state of the edit: the draft, the problem (`tooBig`, `changed`, `refused`, `failed`) and the save.
- `src/search/`: the search (slice 7, `t7/search-screen`). `search.ts` calls `search_pages` and `suggest_words` together with the trimmed query and the notebook filter (or null) and gives `found` with its hits (page, notebook, rank 1 or 2, position, excerpt, the marked range in code points, the form also read) and its suggestions, or `tooLong` / `tooManyWords` for the two refusals `22023`, or `error` for any other failure or any row it cannot read; a failure of the suggestion alone is no failure. `readAnchor` reads `anchor_kind` and `anchor` of one position of `page_tokens` with the rights of the user and gives nothing for a box that is not four numbers. `text.ts` cuts an excerpt on its marked range (code points to UTF-16) and replaces the query words the suggestion named (compared lowercased without accents, a part of a hyphenated word included). `found.ts` accepts a position only as 1 to 9 digits. `service.ts` holds the two calls, `queries.ts` the hooks (the cache key `search` is invalidated with the pages), `timing.ts` the 400 ms wait before the field searches by itself. The screen `src/app/search.tsx` takes an optional `notebook` parameter (ignored unless it is one of the user's notebooks): field of 100 characters at most, one chip per notebook, results under « In the text » (rank 1) and « Also read » (rank 2), the word in bold at rank 1, the form read at rank 2, one tap to search the suggestion. A result opens `/page/[id]?found=<position>`: `FoundPhoto` (in the document of a page of text) reads the box of that position, loads the photo in a hidden 1-pixel probe, and shows the photo (at most 320 high) with the box only when the box lies on the photo (`frameOf` in `src/pages/frame.ts`: four finite numbers, the right and bottom edges at most 2 pixels past the photo, none before 0, the box not empty); otherwise the page opens on its text alone. A tap on that photo opens the photo sheet with the same box (`/page/[id]/photo?found=<position>`), drawn inside the zoomed layer. The box is in the pixels of the stored photo `original.jpg`, which the service reads as it is (the app saved it upright with no metadata).
- `src/export/`: the export of a page, a chapter or a notebook as a PDF or a Markdown file (slice 7, `t7/export`), shared through the system sheet. `document.ts` plans an export from the screen's pages (`planPage`, `planChapter`, `planNotebook`: only read pages that have a text and are not in an unsupported script, chapters in screen order, pages newest first inside each section, pages outside a chapter last under their heading) and builds the document: the title and blocks of `displayDocument` over `cleanView` (tags removed, blank lines of B1 dropped unless B2 shows them, skipped for a page edited by the user), or `mathDocument` rows for a page of math. `sources.ts` reads the B2 text of the pages (groups of 50) and, one page at a time (6 in parallel), whether a page has a gap or a deleted word, with the rights of the user. `markdown.ts` writes one paragraph per group of lines (a hard break between lines, a blank line at a gap), every text escaped (`\ ` * _ [ ] < > & ~ | $ #`, the marker of a list or heading at the start of a line, no indentation, no raw HTML or entity ever); a formula keeps its LaTeX between `$` only when it passes `acceptable` and, once its white space is folded to single spaces, holds only letters, digits and the characters `space { } ^ _ = + - * / ( ) . , | : ; ! ? ' < > & and the backslash`, no address (`:/`, `www.`) and no trailing lone backslash (`<` and `>` become `\lt` and `\gt`), otherwise its source is escaped text. `html.ts` embeds at most 8 million characters of drawings (the rest is escaped source) and encodes each distinct drawing once. It writes the PDF's HTML from fixed templates and escaped text only (`&`, `<`, `>`, quotes), with a content security policy `default-src 'none'; img-src data:; style-src 'unsafe-inline'`, no script, no link, no font, no outside resource; a formula is an `img` whose source is the drawing of `tex.ts` percent-encoded as `data:image/svg+xml;utf8,...` (an image runs nothing), sized by finite numbers, or its escaped source in `code` when it was not drawn. `run.ts` is the one flow: nothing to export, over 100 pages for a PDF or 500 for Markdown (`tooMany`), read the sources, build, draw each distinct formula in order (at most 100 formulas a page and 300 an export, the rest stays text), refuse a finished text over 12 million characters for a PDF or 20 million for Markdown (`tooLarge`), save, share; the file is erased at once when nothing was shared, and otherwise left to the share target (Android resolves the share when the chooser closes, a target may read later) until the next export or the next start of the app sweeps every `export-*` folder of the cache; a `cancelled` control stops it between steps, including the reads of `sources.ts`, and an error after a cancel is a cancel. `files.ts` writes into a new folder of the cache (`export-<random>`): the Markdown file, or the PDF of `expo-print` (A4, 595 by 842) moved to its name; the file name is the name of the page, chapter or notebook reduced by `exportFileName` to letters, digits, dot, dash and underscore, 80 characters at most, never starting or ending on a dot, dash or underscore, and at most 200 bytes of UTF-8 (combining marks are kept). `activity.ts` and `components/export-host.tsx` hold the one running export and its wait with a Cancel button (Android back too); `use-export.ts` gives the menu item « Exporter » (PDF or Markdown) of the three menus, and an Alert for `empty`, `tooMany`, `unavailable` and `error`.
- `src/render/`: the rich rendering on the page (slice 9, `t9/screen`). `markdown.ts` parses the Markdown of a stored rendering (60,000 characters at most, else `null`) into blocks of an allow-list: headings 1 to 3, items of two levels (bullets or the number as written; a second level with no item before it is read as a first one), paragraphs, display formulas, and spans of text with bold, italic, or an inline formula. It applies the rule of `checkFidelity` (`render-check.ts`): a unit is a paragraph, a list item with its continuation lines or a heading; a blank line, a line that starts with a mark or a heading closes it; inside a unit the same three global passes (`**`, `*`, `_`, in that order) remove the emphasis marks and flag what they wrap, so the client shows the words and signs the server compared and nothing else (`tests/render-markdown-server.test.ts` compares both on random inputs and times hostile ones). Everything else (HTML, links, images, code, tables, quotes, `####`, escapes) is shown as text; formulas are masked first with placeholder characters picked among the private-use ones the text does not hold. `api.ts` has `renderPage` (calls `render-page` with the page alone: `ready` with its text, `limit`, `failed`, `discarded`, `network` or `error`), `readRendering` and `readRenderings` (50 pages a read) over `page_renderings`, and `renderingFingerprint` (the SHA-256 of the stored text without `<u>` tags, written in `sha256.ts`, equal to the server's). `mode.ts` keeps the choice "faithful" or "enriched" in `expo-secure-store` (key `render-mode`, faithful when unknown). `use-rendering.ts` decides when to call: only when the page asks for the rendering, its words are placed and the stored rendering is not current; one call at most per account, page and fingerprint while the app runs, a failure is remembered (`discarded` for good, the others until the user chooses "enriched" again) and the faithful text stays shown with one neutral sentence. The switch (`render-switch.tsx`) and the body (`rich-body.tsx`) are in `src/components/reading/`; touching the text, "Edit" or switching the type of the page returns that page to the faithful text without changing the saved choice. The export (`src/export/`) uses the saved choice: in the enriched mode it reads the stored renderings of the pages (never calls the model) and writes a page from its rendering only when the rendering is current, with the headings relative to the title of the page.
- `src/export/data/`: the export of "my data" from the account screen (slice 7, `t7/my-data`), assembled on the phone with the rights of the user: no function, no signed address beyond one photo at a time. `read.ts` reads an allow-list of columns (`EXPORTED_COLUMNS`, checked against `my-data-scope.test.sql` by `export-data-columns.test.ts`), 1,000 rows a request (`range`, at most 200,000 rows a table), always with `user_id = the user` (`id` for `profiles`) and, for the seven page tables, `page_id in` the group asked for, and drops any row of another page. `archive.ts` plans (`planMyData`: the pages, at most 10,000, and the size estimated from the folders of at most 8 pages in `photos`, scaled to the page count) and writes (`writeMyData`): refuses to start without free space for 1.2 times the estimate plus 50 MB (`noSpace`); then `README.txt` (English then French), `profile.json`, `usage.json` (allowance and charges), `notebooks.json` (notebooks, chapters, lexicon), then the pages 20 at a time (the seven page tables are read once per group): the photos of the page (`original`, `prepared`; one that is gone from storage is listed with `file: null` and counted in `export.json`, any other failure stops the export), `text.txt`, `page.json` (the page row, the pipeline version, the readings B1, B2 and A with model, versions and budget, the positions, the corrections, the text added by hand, the alerts, the photo files), and last `export.json`. A photo is fetched only when its path starts with `{user_id}/{page_id}/`. `zip.ts` writes the archive with `fflate` into one file handle, entry by entry (text deflated, photos stored in 256 KB chunks), refuses any name outside its allow-list or twice, and stops with `ArchiveTooLargeError` past 3.5 GB. `service.ts` binds the real parts: a photo is downloaded from a signed address (10 minutes) into a folder `export-photo-*`, must be a JPEG of at most 12 MB, and is erased once written; the archive folder is `export-*`; the sheet is the system's (`application/zip`). `use-my-data.ts` runs the flow: wait, plan, an Alert with the pages and the approximate size, wait with a progress bar (`ExportHost`, which the account screen also embeds because it is a modal) and a Cancel button, then the sheet. A cancel, an error, a full disk or no sheet erases the folder; once shared it stays until the next export or the next start of the app.
- `src/components/account/`: the groups and rows of the account screen.
- `src/components/reading/`: the screens' parts for a reading: ink stroke, waiting view, document, original photo with its zoom, page rows and cards. Under the title of a read document, a page of text or of math shows the "math page" switch, which calls `set-page-type` and comes back to the stored type when the call fails; a page in an unsupported script shows instead a sentence saying the reading AI alone reads it. The body of a page of math is drawn with its formulas (`formula.tsx`, through `react-native-svg`, labelled with its LaTeX for screen readers), the rest of the text in Source Serif 4; a page of text keeps its LaTeX as it was read. On a page of text that is read, every word that `placeWords` can place is tappable (`piece-text.tsx`): a tap marks it and opens `word-editor.tsx` above the keyboard (lifted by `keyboard-lift.tsx`, which reads where the keyboard starts on the screen and pads the screen by the part that covers it, on Android edge to edge as on iOS, with no `KeyboardAvoidingView` and no header height; the page is scrolled so the touched word stays above the bar; a field, Cancel, OK, and the readings of A, B1 and B2 as suggestions); a suggestion is saved with its source as `chosen_from`, a typed word as `keyboard`, a word left as it is is not saved, and a refused or failed save keeps the field open with a sentence. Switching a page to math asks first when the page has corrections, or when the app cannot tell. A read page of text whose words are all placed, or of math, shows "Edit" (`page-text-edit.tsx`): the text becomes one field that fills the screen above Cancel and Save and scrolls itself to the cursor, above the keyboard, the sentence of a problem under them; the alerts of the page are one neutral sentence each under the title. Switching a page to math or back asks first when the page holds corrections or text of the user (words read with the page, for a page of math too).
- `src/theme.ts`: colors, fonts and type sizes of the design direction.
- `tests/`: Jest tests. `tests/fixtures/b1-line-shapes.json` holds only the shape of the lines of the 18 B1 readings of the Thinking Spike (every letter is `x` or `X`, every digit `0`), with the verdict of the ground truth for each line break; no text of a page may enter the repository. `tests/google-keys.test.ts` reads every file git would send (tracked, or new and not ignored) and fails on a Google key or identifier, naming the file and the line, never the value.
- `supabase/migrations/`: the only way the schema changes.
- `supabase/templates/`: the e-mail templates (sign-up, sign-in, confirmation). Each one shows `{{ .Token }}`, the code, and never a link; `config.toml` references them and sets the code at 6 digits, valid 10 minutes, a new one after 30 seconds, and the proof of the address before an account keeps it.
- `supabase/functions/`: Edge Functions (`prepare-page`, `read-page`, `relaunch-page`, `sweep-alto`, `set-page-type`, `delete-account`, `transfer-account`, `delete-page`, `delete-notebook`, `delete-chapter`, `move-page`, `edit-page`, `render-page`), their shared code in `_shared/`, their Deno tests in `tests/`. The last four take the owner from the verified token alone, and a body of exactly their ids (`page_id`; `notebook_id`; `chapter_id`; `page_id`, `notebook_id` and `chapter_id`, null for no chapter), nothing else. `delete-page` and `delete-notebook` read the state first (not found: 404; a page `processing`: 409 `page_processing`, before any file is touched), erase the folder `{user_id}/{page_id}/` of every page in `photos` and `alto` by listing it (whatever the files are named), call the SQL function (`delete_page`, `delete_notebook`), which rechecks under lock, then erase `photos` and `alto` once more, for the pages listed and for the ids `delete_notebook` answers (an A that ended or a photo that landed meanwhile, a page that came into the notebook after the list; a failure there is logged `files_left`, the deletion stands). `delete-chapter` and `move-page` touch no file. Answers: 200 `{deleted: true}` or `{moved: true|false}`, 400 `invalid_request`, 401, 404 `page_not_found`, `notebook_not_found` or `chapter_not_found`, 409 `page_processing`; logs carry a page id at most. `set-page-type` switches a read page of its caller (the owner from the verified token alone) between `text` and `math`; the body holds exactly `page_id` and `page_type` (`text` or `math`, never `unsupported_script`). To math, `set_page_math` (server only, the page row locked) erases A, its positions and the language and answers the ALTO path, which the function erases (a failure is logged `alto_left`, the switch stands). To text, `set_page_text` sets the type and answers B1 (its one `B1` reading), and A is read again in the background from the stored photo, as in `read-page`: no charge, no Gemini. The stored B2 of the page (its one `B2` reading, read beside the function with the service key) goes to `/arbitrate` with B1. A is read again at most once in ten minutes and three times in all for a page (`pages.a_relaunches`, `pages.a_relaunched_at`, written under the same lock, never by the client); past that the type still switches and no A starts (`a_abandoned relaunch_limit`); without a reading service configured, the switch counts nothing (`a_abandoned no_service`). A photo that is missing, not a JPEG or cannot be downloaded leaves the page text without A, and the switch is answered 200. A page not read, in an unsupported script or of another owner is refused; a switch to the type the page has changes nothing. An A that ends after a switch to math is refused by `record_reading_a` (`not_text`). `read-page` and `relaunch-page` share one reading (`_shared/reading.ts`): once the page is claimed, its photo goes at the same time to Gemini (B1), to Gemini again (B2, `_shared/reading-b2.ts`: the same call on its own, so neither reading receives the other nor A, and it is never charged) and to `/recognize` of the reading service (A, `_shared/reading-a.ts`). B1 decides the type of the page (`_shared/page-type.ts`: `math` when it holds a LaTeX delimiter or math command, `$…$` only on one line and when the opening `$` is followed and the closing `$` preceded by a character that is not a space, `$$…$$` when it holds a character that is not a space, `unsupported_script` when most of its letters are not Latin, else `text`), written with `read` in one update; the answer goes back without waiting for A or B2, which go on in the background (`EdgeRuntime.waitUntil`). A B2 that fails is tried once more, then the page stays without one; it is written to `readings` (`label = B2`) only once B1 is stored, on a page of text or of math (cancelled on `unsupported_script`, and dropped when B1 fails, its retry skipped), also when it comes after the answer. Once B2 is written, and when `set-page-type` switches a page to math, `_shared/math-check.ts` checks the page of math for a missing statement (`checkMathPage`, in the background, never an error): it reads B1 and B2 back through `math_check_readings` (server only, empty unless the page is math and has one B1 and one B2), cuts each into statements and normalizes them as `Math Spike/scripts/units.py` does (statement closed by a relation followed by a balanced operand, LaTeX, superscripts, dashes, brackets and spaces unified, plus the exponent or index in braces of one to three characters read as without, and a statement with no letter, digit or empty set left out), compares the two as multisets with no position, and, when one pass has a statement the other lacks, calls `record_math_alert` (server only, the page row locked, math only) with two counts, `only_b1` and `only_b2`, no text. It compares nothing above 60,000 characters a pass or past a work budget of 3,000,000 normalized characters (logged `math_checked` with `skipped`, never an error); the log carries `complete`, `incomplete` or `skipped` and the page id, never a statement. `set_page_text` erases the `math_incomplete` alert. `/arbitrate` gets `b2` when B2 was written, waited for until the budget of A minus the arbitrate timeout (30 s) is spent (`b2_late`), else without. On a text page A and B1 go to `/arbitrate`; on any other type, or when B1 fails, A is cancelled and nothing is written. A failure that may pass (network, timeout, 429, 5xx) is tried once more, a refusal never; then the page stays with B1 alone. `_shared/reading-service.ts` is the only code that calls the service: the secret in `X-Reading-Secret`, no redirect, https, or http only when `SUPABASE_URL` is itself http (the local stack), to `localhost`, `127.0.0.1`, `::1` or a name without a dot, and with no proxy of the environment on the way; the budget of A is 140 s from the send, a timeout of `/recognize` or of `/arbitrate` is never tried again (the call goes on in the service); the answer of `/recognize` passed on as it came. Without `READING_URL` and `READING_SECRET` the page is read by B1 alone; with one of them only, a secret under 32 characters or with spaces around it, an address that is not an origin alone or not https (outside the local stack), or a proxy that would see the secret, the page is read by B1 alone too and the start logs `reading_service_misconfigured` with a fixed code, never a value. `sweep-alto` is called each night at 02:30 UTC by the `pg_cron` task `sweep-alto` (`private.sweep_alto`, its secret and address `sweep_secret` and `sweep_url` in Vault, never the relaunch ones; the address must be https, or http to the local stack, and end in `/functions/v1/sweep-alto`), with its own secret in `X-Sweep-Secret` (`SWEEP_SECRET`, 32 characters at least, no space around it, compared in constant time) and a body of exactly `{}`; it erases from `alto` alone the files `alto_orphans` (server only) names: in `alto`, uploaded and last written more than 24 hours ago, shaped `{uuid}/{uuid}/{file}`, and named by no reading, the user folder left aside (a transfer renames the reading before it copies the file), 1,000 names a call at most; it goes by batches of 500, 20 at most and stops after a minute, or when the storage removed fewer than asked; it never touches `photos`. Answers: 200 `{removed: n}`, 400 `invalid_request`, 401, 405; it logs a count and a duration only, also when a batch fails after others. Local secrets (`GEMINI_API_KEY`, `RELAUNCH_SECRET`, `SWEEP_SECRET`, `READING_URL`, `READING_SECRET`) live in `supabase/functions/.env`, ignored by git. The `pg_cron` tasks that call `relaunch-page` and `sweep-alto` read their secret and address from Supabase Vault, never from the repository. The `pg_cron` task `purge-cron-history` (`private.purge_cron_history`, executable by nobody, every night at 02:45 UTC) erases the runs of `cron.job_run_details` that ended more than 30 days ago, and keeps a run still going.
- `edit-page` (same folder) takes the owner from the verified token alone and a body of exactly `page_id`, `text`, `words` (at most 2,000 of exactly `token_index` and `text`, at most 100 characters, empty to delete a word, no index twice) and `gaps` (at most 20,001 of exactly `gap_index` and `text`, no index twice, their texts together no longer than `text`); `text` is at most 200,000 characters and the body 2,000,000 (checked on `content-length` first); no text, word or gap holds a NUL or a lone surrogate. It only passes them to `edit_page` (server only), which takes the lock of the page, checks them again, inserts each word as a correction from the keyboard, writes the gaps (and drops those equal to what B1 had), rebuilds the text once (the trigger of corrections is told to wait by a transaction-local setting only `edit_page` sets) and refuses with `text_mismatch`, undoing everything, unless the text is exactly `text`. Answers: 200 `{edited: true}`, 400 `invalid_request`, 401, 404 `page_not_found`, 409 `page_not_read`, `unsupported_script`, `not_editable` (a page of text with no place for its words) or `text_changed`, 422 `invalid_edit`; logs carry a page id at most.
- `render-page` (same folder, `verify_jwt = true`, slice 9) takes the owner from the verified token alone and a body of exactly `page_id`. It reads the page, its text and its rendering with the service key, always filtered by the owner (`createRenderStore`), and refuses: 404 `page_not_found` (also another user's page), 409 `page_not_read`, `unsupported_script` or `no_text`, 413 `text_too_long`. The input is the stored text without the `<u>` and `</u>` tags (`renderInput`) and nothing else (no photo, readings, notebook name or the blank-line rule of `clean.ts`, which needs B2), at most 20,000 characters after the tags are dropped. Its fingerprint is the SHA-256 of that string. When `page_renderings.based_on` equals it, the stored rendering is answered with no charge and no call. Otherwise one `charge_render` (a rendering of the day, 5 at most, never the monthly limit), then one call to `gemini-3.6-flash` with `thinkingBudget: 0`, the prompt `r1` as system instruction, the input alone as content and `maxOutputTokens: 32768` (`_shared/gemini-render.ts`); a Gemini failure is tried once more, except a 429 which is never tried again, then answered `failed` with nothing stored. Simultaneous calls on the same user, page and fingerprint share one task in the isolate, so one charge and one call. The output is accepted only if `checkFidelity` (`_shared/render-check.ts`) finds the same words and signs once the accepted marks are removed from both sides: `#`, `##`, `###`, `-`, `*`, `+` or `1.` at the start of a line (up to 4 spaces of indent), `**x**`, `*x*` and `_x_` (500 characters at most) around a text that starts and ends with a character that is not a space or the mark, after the formulas (`$…$`, `$$…$$`, `\(…\)`, `\[…\]`, `egin…\end`) are set aside on both sides, then every run of spaces and line breaks reduced to one space; the emphasis marks are removed per unit (a paragraph, a list item with its continuation lines, or a heading: a blank line, a line that starts with a mark or a heading closes the unit), so an emphasis may span the lines of a unit but not a paragraph break. The output must keep at least as many headings, bullets and emphasis marks as the input held, an input `N.` must come back unchanged and a numbered list the model adds must count from 1 without a gap. Before writing, the function reads the page again and discards the rendering (`page_changed`) when the text is no longer the one it rendered. It is closed by comparison, not by a list: any added character (HTML, link, image, code, table, quote, escape, a mark not closed or not at a line start) makes the two differ and the rendering is discarded; the logged reason is `output_too_long` (over 60,000 characters), `refused_mark` (more `<`, `>`, `[`, backtick, `|` or `\` than the input) or `text_changed`. Answers, all 200: `{status: "ready", text}`, `{status: "limit"}` (past 5, no call, a stored current rendering is still served first), `{status: "failed"}`, `{status: "discarded"}` (charged, never stored). Logs carry `render_done`, `render_cached`, `render_discarded` (with the reason), `render_failed` and `render_limit`, a page id and token counts, never text.

- `services/reading/`: the reading service. `src/reading/segmentation.py` cuts a photo into lines with the chain of the Frame Spike, every setting unchanged: Kraken's `blla` finds baseline fragments, fragments of the same height make one row, `ink.py` keeps the ink components under a threshold set from the page's own ink (so a printed grid goes and a light pencil stroke stays), each component goes to its row (a component across two rows is cut), and each row becomes one straightened grey tile at most 1,700 px wide, its neighbours' ink erased, rows in order from the top. Each line keeps its ink box on the photo and the placement that carries a point of the tile back to the photo (continuous coordinates: the centre of pixel `(u, v)` is `(u + 0.5, v + 0.5)`). `blla` runs on the processor and on one thread, as `kraken segment` does by default, so the lines of a photo do not depend on the number of cores; the pinned tiles of the spikes came from `blla` on a graphics card, so 77 of their 310 tiles differ slightly from the service's (measure in `04 Testing/T2 mesures/segmentation/`, outside the repository). The thread limit is process-wide: keep segmentation off threads that run other work. `src/reading/recognition.py` reads each tile as one whole line with `medium` on the processor, one thread, as `kraken ocr --no-segmentation` did for the Cold Spike; a word is a run between spaces, its box runs from the cut of its first character to the cut of its last (one tile pixel wide for a one-letter word), carried onto the photo, and its confidence is the mean of its characters rounded to 4 decimals, as Kraken's ALTO writes it. `Reader` reads one page at a time. `POST /recognize?page_id=<lowercase uuid>` takes the photo as the raw body with exactly one `Content-Type: image/jpeg`, and nothing else (any other query field or body type is refused), and answers `page_id`, `width`, `height`, `lines` (`row`, `box` of the tile on the photo, `ink` box, `words` with `text`, `box`, `confidence`) and `alto`; it writes nothing. Refusals have fixed bodies: 400 `invalid_request` or `invalid_photo` (not baseline or progressive, more than 16 scans, broken), 413 `too_large` (above 10 MB, 12 Mpx or 8,000 px a side), 415 `unsupported_media_type`, 422 `too_complex` (more than 40,000 ink components, or more than 120 s of reading), 503 `busy` (four requests already in flight), 500 `reading_failed`. `POST /arbitrate` takes exactly one `Content-Type: application/json` and a body of exactly `page_id`, `user_id` (lowercase uuids), `b1` (the text B1 shows), `b2` (optional, the text of B2, bounded as `b1`), `a` (the answer of `/recognize` as it came, its ALTO checked against the ALTO its lines give) and `duration_ms`; it needs no Kraken work and does not wait for a reading. `src/reading/tokens.py` cuts a text into words as `Disagreement Spike/scripts/tokens.py` does (same matches, same words left out), but keeps each word as written in B1 (case, apostrophes, hyphens) and compares a form where apostrophes and dashes are unified, case kept. `src/reading/alignment.py` is the block aligner of `Signal Spike/scripts/align_blocks.py` (`align_free`), unchanged: B1 is the pivot, a line `/recognize` cut but read empty is a declared hole, a hole costs nothing and absorbs at most the surplus of words of B1 over A (and the length of as many of B1's longest lines); a page above 1,000,000 cells of the table, or whose letters of B1 times letters of A pass 50,000,000, answers 422 `too_complex`. `src/reading/arbitration.py` gives one position per word of B1: its rank, its line in B1, the word of A paired with it, and its anchor: the box of that word of A; else the `box` of the line of A nearest on the same line of B1; else, in a hole, the line of the hole (one line of B1 per line of the hole, or the box spanning the hole when their numbers differ); else none. The gate is open only on an `active` personal model whose `cer` is at most 0.129; closed, no rule runs and `rule` and `channel` stay empty. `src/reading/rules.py` holds the rules, gate open, in the order 2, 5, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 of the Arbitrage (rule 1 waits for slice 5): the type is the strictest of A, B1 and B2 (a digit in any makes a number; a proper noun flagged by the entities port on B1, on the word of A paired with it or on the word of B2 paired with it); readings are compared in their form (case kept, apostrophes and dashes unified); rule 2 (number read differently) writes B1 to `queue`; rule 5 (B1 and B2 differ and A confirms one of them) writes the word A confirms, as that pass read it, to `layer`; rule 3 (proper noun) B1 to `layer`; rule 4 (common word, A at a confidence of at least 0.990, one edit, homophones by the phonemes port, not an accent alone, not a truncation) A on a `journal` notebook, else B1, to `layer`; rule 6 (B1 and B2 differ and A confirms neither, or read nothing there) B1 to `queue`; rule 7 (the word is on a line B2 lacks) B1 to `none`, the alert being the page's; rule 8 (any other disagreement) B1 to `layer`; rule 9 (A and B1 agree, whatever B2 reads) and rule 10 (A read nothing there) B1 to `none`. Without B2, rules 5, 6 and 7 stay silent. `SecondRefusedError` is raised when the entities port refuses B2 (over 15,000 sub-tokens): the arbiter then decides the page without B2. Language tiers: French runs the entities and rule 4 in French; English, rule 4 in English and no entities; any other language, or none found, neither. `src/reading/phonemes.py` is espeak-ng through `phonemizer` (`espeakng-loader`, `fr-fr` and `en-us`, no stress, `language_switch="remove-utterance"`: a word espeak reads in another language has no phonemes and is no homophone), one call at a time, logging nothing. `src/reading/replay.py` (`python -m reading.replay` in the image) replays readings through the rules with the gate forced open: JSON on stdin (pages with B1, optionally B2, A's lines of words and confidences, language, level, and the entity flags of B1, of A and, with B2, of B2 pinned), positions on stdout; a bad input exits 2 with `invalid replay input`, never its content. The arbiter reads the correction level of the page's notebook only when the gate is open. `src/reading/entities.py` is the entities port: `distilcamembert-base-ner` in ONNX on the processor, one thread, read in windows of 510 sub-tokens on the form of the text (as the NER Spike did), `<` and `>` and their full-width and small-form look-alikes read as spaces, and any special id left after encoding read as `<unk>`, so that no special token can be written on a page, and above 15,000 sub-tokens for B1 or for A the arbitration answers 422 `too_complex`; a word is a proper noun when the first sub-token over its characters carries `PER`, `LOC`, `ORG` or `MISC`; one flag per token of `line_tokens`. onnxruntime 1.30 sends telemetry to Microsoft unless `ORT_DISABLE_TELEMETRY=1` is set before it loads (the API switch comes too late): the image sets it, `Camembert` refuses to start without it and imports onnxruntime only after that check, and a test proves no connection opens. The service wires the rules at start (`Espeak` and `Camembert`) and does not start without `READING_NER`, the folder of the NER files. `src/reading/language.py` finds the language of B1 with lingua (all its languages, minimum relative distance 0.25), none under five words with a letter. `src/reading/second.py` ranges B2 by content before the aligner: `b2_content_map` of the Pairing Probe, same steps over the forms (case kept, apostrophes and dashes unified), then the global aligner of `Disagreement Spike/scripts/tokens.py` (`align_global`), so that each word of B1 has the word of B2 read there, or none; on 399 random pages and on the 52 stored pairs of real readings of the spikes it pairs exactly as the spike. A line of B1 (or of B2) is missing when none of its words is paired and its run of words is nowhere in the other pass. B2 is left out when it has no word, when it passes 1,000,000 cells or 50,000,000 letter pairs with B1, or when the search passes 20,000,000 steps (a page of identical words): the page is read as without B2, never refused. `src/reading/tokens.py` also gives `raw_spans`, the place of each word in the characters of the text as it was written (the NFC form `tokens_of` reads can be shorter); none for the whole page when a word would end inside a composed character. `src/reading/arbiter.py` reads the user's models, computes the positions and the language, gives each position its place in B1 (`b1_start`, `b1_end`) and the word B2 read there (`reading_b2`), gate open or not, and raises the `missing_line` alert of the page when one pass lacks a whole line (`absent_from_b2`, `absent_from_b1`: the indexes of those lines, 50 at most each, no text), gate open or not, uploads the ALTO to `alto/{user_id}/{page_id}/a-<32 hex>.xml` (a new name for each attempt), then calls `record_reading_a` (model `medium`, version `1`, the alerts in `p_alerts` only when there are some). It erases that file when the write is refused (409 with `page_not_found`, `not_text` or `already_read`) or surely failed (400, 401, 403, 404 or 409); after a failure that may have written (network, timeout, any other status) it leaves the file and logs `alto=left`. `src/reading/store.py` is the only code that calls Supabase: `htr_models`, the `correction_level` of a page's notebook (read through `pages`), the `alto` bucket and `record_reading_a`, with the service key, never `page_texts`, over https only (http only to `localhost`, `127.0.0.1`, `::1` or the stack's `supabase_kong_*` gateway, a name without a dot), following no redirect and no proxy of the environment. It answers 200 with `page_id`, `outcome` (`recorded`) and `tokens`, or 400 `invalid_request`, 409, 413 `too_large` (above 12 MB), 415, 422 `too_complex`, 503 `busy` (four arbitrations in flight), 500 `arbitration_failed`. Logs carry the page, durations and counts, never text or image. `pyproject.toml` and `uv.lock` pin every package: the Kraken chain keeps the versions that produced the Cold Spike (`constraint-dependencies`), torch comes from the PyTorch index for the processor. The `Dockerfile` downloads the `medium` model from Zenodo (record 21788410, Apache-2.0) and checks its SHA-256, and the NER the same way from Hugging Face at pinned revisions: `model.onnx` and `config.json` of `cmarkea/distilcamembert-base-ner` (MIT), `tokenizer.json` of `Xenova/camembert-ner` (the CamemBERT tokenizer the NER Spike used), into `/models/ner`; Kraken carries its `blla` segmenter; the service runs as a user that is not root and cannot change its model. Every route requires the shared secret in the `X-Reading-Secret` header, compared in constant time, and `Authorization` stays free for the identity token of Cloud Run; without a `READING_SECRET` of at least 32 characters the service does not start. Nor does it start without `SUPABASE_URL` (as above) and `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`. `check.sh` runs ruff, mypy, pytest and pip-audit inside the image; `audit-allowlist.txt` lists the advisories pip-audit ignores. ## Data rules in detail

- The client deletes no row and changes neither `pages.notebook_id` nor `pages.chapter_id`: a page is moved and a page, a chapter or a notebook is deleted only by `move_page`, `delete_page`, `delete_chapter` and `delete_notebook` (server only, the owner given by the verified token). Each answers an outcome (`moved`, `unchanged`, `deleted`, `page_not_found`, `chapter_not_found`, `notebook_not_found`, `page_processing`; `delete_notebook` also answers the ids of the pages it deleted), refuses a page being read, and takes the advisory lock of the user first (the one `charge_page` and the transfer take), then its row locks notebook first, then chapter, then pages by id (`supabase/tests/concurrency/library-race.sh` plays them all at once). A chapter name is unique in its notebook; deleting a chapter keeps its pages, out of any chapter; deleting a notebook deletes its pages, and the charges of deleted pages stay with no page. The functions touch no file: the Edge Function erases the files first. A transfer merges chapters of the same name like notebooks. Every foreign key `(…, user_id)` has an index starting with its columns.
- Reading A is written only by `record_reading_a` (server only): the reading, its positions in `page_tokens`, `pages.language` and the `missing_line` alert of the page (`p_alerts`: no other kind, one of a kind a page, one already there kept as it is), in one transaction, the page row locked. It answers `recorded`, or refuses with `page_not_found` (no such page, or another owner), `not_text` or `already_read`; on a refusal or an error the caller erases the ALTO file it uploaded. A reading A has an `alto_path` (a file name directly in its page folder) and none of the settings of a Gemini call, B1 and B2 the reverse; a page has one A at most. A page leaves `text` only once its A and its positions are erased (trigger `private.check_page_type`).
- Corrections and page alerts (slice 4). `page_tokens` also holds `reading_b2`, `review_status` (`pending`, `confirmed`, `corrected`, `skipped`, only for a position of the `queue` channel, which starts `pending`) and the place of the word in the raw text of B1 (`b1_start` included, `b1_end` excluded, in characters; a place never overlaps another and follows the rank, a page has all its places or none; the server writes them through `record_reading_a`, whose token objects may carry `reading_b2`, `b1_start` and `b1_end`). The text a page shows (`page_texts.text`) is rebuilt by `private.recompute_page_text` from the B1 reading, the places and, for each position, its last correction, else the word a rule wrote, else the slice of B1 as it was, so that line breaks and punctuation never change; a page whose positions have no place keeps its text. The client writes `corrections` (insert of `page_id`, `token_index`, `corrected_text`, `chosen_from`, `from_queue` only, no update, no delete): `private.prepare_correction` locks the page, gives the `revision` (1 to 100 a position, 2,000 corrections a page) and `previous_text`, keeps `from_queue` only on a pending position, and refuses a word that is not one word of 1 to 100 characters (no space, control, bidirectional or invisible character from its fixed list), a position that has no place, and a `chosen_from` of `A`, `B1` or `B2` whose word is not exactly that reading of the position; `private.apply_correction` moves a pending queue position to `confirmed` or `corrected` and recalculates the text. Undoing a correction adds one that returns to the previous word. `page_alerts` (`date_incoherent`, `missing_line`, `math_incomplete`, one of each kind a page, `details` an object of 16 KB at most) is written by the server only (`math_incomplete` only by `record_math_alert`, which refuses a page that is not math or lacks B1 or B2, and counts that are negative or both zero). A page has at most one reading B2 (`readings_one_b2_per_page`). `set_page_math` erases the positions (and with them the corrections), the `missing_line` alert and A, and gives the text of B1 back. `supabase/tests/concurrency/corrections-race.sh` plays 20 corrections at once on one page.
- Text of the user (slice 4, `t4/edit`). `page_gaps` (`page_id`, `gap_index`, `text` of 100,000 characters at most; read by its owner, written by the server only) holds the text between the words of a page: gap `n` is what comes before the position of rank `n` (counted from 0), the last gap what comes after the last position, and on a page of math, which has no position, the whole text in gap 0. `private.recompute_page_text` takes the gap in place of the slice of B1 when there is one, and rebuilds a page of math from its gap 0 or from B1. A correction may be empty (a deleted word) only when the server writes it (`auth.uid()` empty): a client's empty correction is refused. `edit_page(page, owner, text, words, gaps)` (server only, owner given by the verified token) is the only way the text of the user is written, and `set_page_math` and `set_page_text` erase it and show B1 again. `supabase/tests/concurrency/edit-race.sh` plays edits and corrections at once on one page (like `corrections-race.sh`).
- Search (slice 7, `t7/search-db`). `public.search_pages(p_query, p_notebook_id default null)` is the one function the client calls to search; it runs with the rights of its owner and an empty search path, and keeps `user_id = auth.uid()` on every table by hand (no account, no query or an empty one: no rows). Words come from the text search configuration `public.search_simple` (the `simple` configuration, accents removed by `unaccent`, case ignored, the whole of a hyphenated word dropped so only its parts count, tags never words), so a query is cut into its distinct words in order. Refused with `22023`: a query over 100 characters (`query_too_long`) or of more than 8 distinct words (`query_too_many_words`); a query with no word finds nothing. A page is found when every word is in its shown text (`page_texts.text`, rank 1) or in the shown text or another reading of it (`reading_a`, `reading_b1`, `reading_b2` of a position, or the `raw_text` of its B1 or B2, rank 2), a page of rank 1 never comes again at rank 2, and a page of math is found at rank 1 only. Only pages whose `uploaded_at` is set and that have a shown text; a notebook given (or not the caller's) narrows the search. At most 50 pages an answer, rank 1 first, the most recent `captured_at` first in each rank. Each row has `page_id`, `notebook_id`, `rank`, `token_index` (the first position that carries the first word of rank 1, or the first word the shown text lacks at rank 2: the shown word of a position is its last correction, else the word a rule wrote; null without such a position), `excerpt` (4,000 characters at most), `match_start` and `match_end` (in characters, `match_end` excluded, of the word in the excerpt, null when none is marked) and `also_read`. Rank 1: the excerpt is the line of the shown text that carries the word, marked. Rank 2 with a position: the line of the shown text at the place of the position (counted in newlines of B1, only when the shown text has as many lines as B1), else the line of B1 at that place or at `line_index`, else the form itself; nothing marked; `also_read` is the form the other reading wrote. Rank 2 without a position: the line of B1 or B2 that carries the word, marked, `also_read` the form as written there. Full-text indexes: `page_texts_search_idx`, `page_tokens_search_idx` (the three readings of a position together) and `readings_search_idx` (B1 and B2 only). Known limits of the parser, kept: text between `<` and `>` is read as a tag and its words are not indexed, and a missing space after a dot (`Mr.Magellan`) makes one `host` or `file` word. `supabase/tests/database/search.test.sql` covers it.
- `pages.page_type` (`text` by default) and `pages.language` (two lowercase letters, or nothing) are written by the server only: by `read-page` and `relaunch-page` with B1, and by `set_page_math` and `set_page_text` (server only) through `set-page-type`. `notebooks.correction_level` stays `course` unless its owner has an `active` personal model in `htr_models` (trigger `private.check_correction_level`), and the client never writes it. `htr_models` holds the generic `medium` (no owner, no file: it is in the image) and, from slice 5, personal models; `cer` is a fraction (0.129 is 12.9 %).
- Lexicon (slice 7, `t7/lexicon`). `public.lexicon_entries` (`notebook_id`, `user_id`, `form_key`, `form`, `occurrences`, `confirmed`; key `(notebook_id, form_key)`) is read by its owner and written by the server alone, by `private.refresh_lexicon`. A notebook's lexicon is always the result of `private.lexicon_notebook(notebook_id)`, a full recomputation over the pages of the notebook that have a shown text and are not of type `math`: every word of the shown text (tags removed, cut at hyphens and apostrophes, parts of one letter, without a letter, over 40 characters, or ordinal like `1er` left out; a hyphenated or elided word that is whole in the dictionary left out) is a candidate form when it is absent from `private.dictionary_words`, or starts with a capital without being the first word of its line or following `.`, `!`, `?` or `…`, or carries both a letter and a digit, or is followed by a full stop and then a lowercase letter or a digit. A form enters from two pages that carry it, or from one when `confirmed`: its key is among the words of the last correction of a position of the notebook or of a `page_gaps` text. `form_key` is the form in lowercase without accents, `form` the spelling that appears most often (ties: the smallest). At most 5,000 forms a notebook (confirmed first, then the most frequent). Pages read, corrected, edited, moved, switched to or from math, erased, and notebooks erased or merged by a transfer queue the notebook in `private.lexicon_pending` (triggers on `page_texts`, `corrections`, `page_gaps`, `pages`); a deferred constraint trigger on that table recomputes each queued notebook once, when the operation ends, under the advisory lock `lexicon:<notebook id>` taken at that moment, after the page locks of the operation. The recomputation takes a key-share lock on the notebook row (foreign key), so `delete_notebook` and `transfer_account_rows` lock notebooks `for no key update`, and `transfer_account_rows` takes the lexicon locks of the notebooks that move before it touches their lexicon rows. A word of more than 40 characters is never read. The migration queues every notebook that already has a page text, so existing notebooks get their lexicon when it is applied. `transfer_account_rows` moves the lexicon rows of the notebooks that move; a merged notebook is recomputed. `private.dictionary_words` (about 400,000 lowercase words: `wfrench` 1.2.9 and the lowercase entries of `wamerican` 2020.12.07, built by `supabase/dictionary/build.mjs`; licences in `docs/dictionary.md`) is unreachable by the client and written by no function. `public.suggest_words(p_query, p_notebook_id default null)` takes the bounds of `search_pages` (same refusals `22023`), and gives, for each word of the query of at least 4 characters that is in no shown text of the searched pages (those with `uploaded_at`), the form of the lexicon of the caller closest to it by trigram similarity of at least 0.5 (ties: confirmed, then more occurrences, then key), as `(word, suggestion)` where `word` is the word of the query lowercased without accents. `supabase/tests/database/lexicon.test.sql`, `lexicon-transfer.test.sql` and `supabase/tests/concurrency/lexicon-race.sh` cover it.
- Renderings (slice 9, `t9/schema`). `public.page_renderings` (`page_id` primary key, `user_id`, `text` Markdown of 1 to 60,000 characters, `based_on` the SHA-256 fingerprint of the input text in 64 lowercase hexadecimal characters, `model`, `prompt_version`, `created_at`) holds at most one rendering per page, with the composite foreign key `(page_id, user_id)` to `pages` (cascade, deferrable): it goes with its page, with the account, and follows `transfer_account_rows`. The owner reads it (`user_id = auth.uid()`); only the server writes it. A rendering is current when `based_on` equals the fingerprint of the shown text; nothing deletes it when the text changes. `public.render_daily_counts` (`user_id`, `day`, `renders` from 0 to 5, key `(user_id, day)`) is readable and writable by the server alone (RLS on, no policy). `public.charge_render(p_user_id)` (server only, owner's rights, empty search path) takes one rendering of the day, the day being the Paris date: it deletes the user's rows of earlier days, then adds one if the count is under 5 and answers `(true, used)`, else answers `(false, 5)` without counting; a null user raises `account_not_found`, an unknown user violates the foreign key (`23503`). The caller calls it before the model, so discarded renderings count; it touches neither `page_charges` nor the monthly limit. `transfer_account_rows` moves the renderings and adds the counts of the giving account to those of the registered one per day (capped at 5). `supabase/tests/database/page-renderings.test.sql` and `page-renderings-transfer.test.sql` cover it, `supabase/tests/concurrency/charge-render.sh` the count under 40 simultaneous calls.
